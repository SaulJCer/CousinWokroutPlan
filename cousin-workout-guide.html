Workout days

Repeat the same plan each week.

Day 1

Push + legs

Bench Sit-to-Stand, Dumbbell Bench Press, Leg Extension, Wall Push-Up, Front Plank on Bench.

Day 2

Pull + simple movement

One-Arm Row, Glute Bridge, Farmer Carry, Calf Raise, Dead Bug.

Day 3

Lower body + upper push

Goblet Box Squat, Incline Push-Up, Step-Up, Seated Shoulder Press, Side Plank from Knees.

Day 4

Pull + basketball support

Chest-Supported Row, Reverse Lunge Hold, Leg Extension, Dumbbell Curl, Fast March in Place.

Workout rules

Simple coaching, safe pacing, and adult help when needed.

Safety

Use light weights, smooth reps, and close adult help for bench, dumbbell, and balance exercises. Youth resistance training guidance supports supervised, age-appropriate training with technique first. [web:92][web:4]

Basketball support

Basketball can count toward the daily 60 minutes of physical activity, and muscle-strengthening activity should be included at least 3 days per week. [web:13][web:20]
