1. Bench Sit-to-Stand

2–3 x 8–10Rest 60 sec
Feet flat on floor
Chest tall
Stand, then sit slow

Harder than expected? Use a higher bench and hands on thighs.

Next Exercise

2. Dumbbell Bench Press

2–3 x 8–10Rest 60–75 secAdult supervision
Feet on floor
Wrists straight
Press up smoothly

Harder than expected? Switch to push-ups against the bench.

Next Exercise

3. Leg Extension

2 x 10–12Rest 45–60 sec
Back against pad
Lift with control
Lower slow

Harder than expected? Try seated knee straightening with no weight.

Next Exercise

4. Wall Push-Up

2 x 8–12Rest 45 sec
Hands on wall
Body straight
Push away strong

Harder than expected? Hold a standing wall lean for time.
